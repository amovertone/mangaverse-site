Manga-Verse: Ink

Support

Manga-Verse: Ink is an inking app for iPad and Apple Pencil, built around Japanese dip pens, brush pens and markers.

What you need

An iPad and an Apple Pencil. To try the app without a Pencil, open Tune and switch Draw with finger on.

Questions and feedback

Tell us which pens feel right and which do not. Email amovertone@gmail.com.

Your data

The app collects nothing and makes no network requests. See the privacy policy.
