Privacy Policy

Manga-Verse: Ink · last updated 18 November 2026

Manga-Verse: Ink collects no personal data. The only information that leaves your iPad is what a purchase needs, and it is not linked to you.

App Store Connect requires a hosted URL and reviewers expect the specifics, so here they are.

What we collect

Your drawings, your pen settings and anything you import never leave your iPad. There is no account system, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.

If you buy a pack or a single pen, the purchase is handled by Apple's App Store and by RevenueCat, a service that tells the app which pens you own. RevenueCat receives your purchase history (which packs and pens you bought, and when) under an anonymous random ID created on your iPad. It does not receive your name, your Apple Account, your email, your drawings or anything else. It is used only to unlock the pens you bought, to restore them if you reinstall the app, and to give us anonymous purchase statistics (such as how many people bought a pack). We do not use it for advertising or tracking, and we do not link it to you.

Network activity

The app connects to the internet only to show the price of a pack or pen in your currency, to complete a purchase, and to restore purchases. It works without a connection: drawing, saving and every pen you own keep working offline. Every image, font and piece of text is bundled inside the app.

What is stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of state locally so it can pick up where you left off:

  • Your drawing: the one page you are working on, saved automatically, including its pencil layer (blue and red lead), which is kept in its own file on the device
  • Which pen you last chose, and the settings you set for each pen
  • Your language choice (English or Japanese) and which hand you draw with
  • Whether "Draw with finger" is switched on
  • The picture you import to trace (Import), which stays on your device until you replace or remove it

This is written to the app's own storage on your device. It never leaves the device, is not readable by us, and is deleted when you delete the app.

Sharing your drawing

The Share button turns your page into a PNG picture and opens the standard iPad share sheet. What happens next is your choice: you pick where it goes (Files, Messages, Photos, AirDrop and so on). The app itself sends nothing and keeps no copy outside the app.

If you choose Save Image, the page is added to your Photos library. The app can add a picture there but cannot read your other photos. When you import a picture to trace, the app sees only the picture you choose in the system photo picker.

Apple Pencil

The app reads Apple Pencil input (position, pressure and tilt) to draw. This information is used only to draw the line on screen, in memory, while you draw. It is not stored beyond your drawing and is never sent anywhere.

In-app purchases

Packs, single pens and Complete Studio are one-time purchases (no subscription), sold through the App Store, with Family Sharing on. Purchases are processed by Apple. RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) is used to know which purchases you have; see revenuecat.com/privacy for how it handles data. If you installed Manga-Verse: Ink before packs existed (build 3 or earlier), the app checks on your device, using Apple's own record of your first install, that you keep every pen; nothing about that check is sent to us.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal data from anyone, children included, and it has no advertising and no tracking.

Third parties

RevenueCat, Inc. (purchase bookkeeping, as described above) and Apple (the App Store). There are no advertising networks and no analytics providers.

Fonts and open-source software

The app bundles open-source fonts (Barlow, Barlow Condensed, JetBrains Mono, Dela Gothic One and Sora), all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Notices are on the licenses page.
