Privacy Policy

Manga-Verse: Ink · last updated 14 November 2026

Manga-Verse: Ink does not collect any data.

That is the whole policy. App Store Connect requires a hosted URL and reviewers expect the specifics, so here they are.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account system, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.

Network activity

The app makes no network requests. Every image, font and piece of text is bundled inside the application and read from the device. The app works identically with no connection at all. Because nothing is sent anywhere, there is no data in transit to protect and no server that holds information about you.

What is stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of state locally so it can pick up where you left off:

  • Your drawing: the one page you are working on, saved automatically, including its pencil layer (blue and red lead), which is kept in its own file on the device
  • Which pen you last chose, and the settings you set for each pen
  • Your language choice (English or Japanese) and which hand you draw with
  • Whether "Draw with finger" is switched on
  • The picture you import to trace (Import), which stays on your device until you replace or remove it

This is written to the app's own storage on your device. It never leaves the device, is not readable by us, and is deleted when you delete the app.

Sharing your drawing

The Share button turns your page into a PNG picture and opens the standard iPad share sheet. What happens next is your choice: you pick where it goes (Files, Messages, Photos, AirDrop and so on). The app itself sends nothing and keeps no copy outside the app.

If you choose Save Image, the page is added to your Photos library. The app can add a picture there but cannot read your other photos. When you import a picture to trace, the app sees only the picture you choose in the system photo picker.

Apple Pencil

The app reads Apple Pencil input (position, pressure and tilt) to draw. This information is used only to draw the line on screen, in memory, while you draw. It is not stored beyond your drawing and is never sent anywhere.

In-app purchases

There are none.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects none from children either.

Third parties

None. The app contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising networks and no analytics providers.

Fonts and open-source software

The app bundles open-source fonts (Barlow, Barlow Condensed, JetBrains Mono, Dela Gothic One and Sora), all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Notices are on the licenses page.

Not affiliated
