Mangaverse

Support & contact

Mangaverse is a small museum of the artists modern manga came from — ten mangaka from Edo through Shōwa, and over four hundred pages of public-domain woodblock prints and illustrated books.

Contact

Email amovertone@gmail.com. I read everything and usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-in. Everything is available the moment you open the app.

Does it work offline? Yes, entirely. Every image and font ships inside the app and it makes no network requests at all.

Where is the artwork from? All of it is public domain, from The Metropolitan Museum of Art (CC0), Wikimedia Commons (PD-art) and the National Diet Library of Japan.

My reading progress disappeared. Progress is stored on your device only, so deleting the app removes it. There is no cloud backup.

Privacy

The app collects nothing. See the privacy policy.
