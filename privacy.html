Privacy Policy

Manga-Verse · last updated 2 September 2026

Manga-Verse does not collect any data. That is the whole policy, but the specifics are below.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account system, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind.

Network activity

The app makes no network requests. Every image, font and piece of text is bundled inside the application and read from the device, so it works identically with no connection. Because nothing is sent anywhere, there is no data in transit and no server holding information about you.

Stored on your device

A small amount of state is saved locally so the app can remember where you were:

  • Which theme is active
  • Reading progress — the page of each work you last read
  • Your display name and reading preferences

This never leaves the device, is not readable by us, and is deleted when you delete the app.

In-app purchases

There are none. All five visual themes are included; nothing is locked and no payment information is ever requested.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects none from children either.

Third parties

None. The app contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks or analytics providers.

Artwork

All artwork is public domain, from open access programs at institutions including The Metropolitan Museum of Art (CC0), Wikimedia Commons (PD-art) and the National Diet Library of Japan. The artists represented died between 1816 and 1957, and only pre-1931 publications were used for the three who died after 1946.

Fonts and open-source software

The app bundles open-source fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1 and Apache License 2.0) and open-source software (including maplibre-gl and pmtiles under the BSD 3-Clause License). The offline map is built from OpenStreetMap data, used under the Open Database License. Full notices are on the licenses page.

Not affiliated

Manga-Verse is an independent application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any manga or anime publisher, studio, or rights holder. Modern works are named only for historical comparison. All product names, brands, and trademarks referenced belong to their respective owners.

Copyright concerns

Every image is believed to be in the public domain and is documented with its source. If you hold rights in any material shown in the app and believe it should not appear, email the address below and it will be reviewed and, if appropriate, removed.
